For a generation, the television test signal marked an interruption: nothing was coming through. In the Intelligence Age, that image returns as a kind of technological nostalgia. We live in systems designed to remain permanently connected, generating, responding, transmitting. ■ of Absence returns to the old signal to expose a simple paradox: even absence needs a signal to be communicated. To say that nothing is there, the system must remain on.
